A GUIDE FOR STUDENTS

How FairAid works.

Before you begin, here's what you'll need, what FairAid checks, and how to make sense of your results.

What to prepareThe screening processUnderstanding resultsWhat happens next
Before you begin

What information will you need?

FairAid asks for five details about your circumstances. You do not need to upload documents to complete the current pre-screening form.

Your NQF level
The qualification level you are studying towards or have reached.
Field of study
Your programme or subject area, selected from the available options.
Annual household income
The total income of your household over a year, in Namibian dollars (N$), not a monthly amount.
Region
Your region, used when a funding scheme has location-related criteria.
Disability status
A yes-or-no response used where a scheme includes disability-related eligibility requirements.

Use accurate information. FairAid compares what you enter with the criteria registered for each scheme.

The assessment

How is eligibility checked?

After signing in, you complete the form and select Run pre-screening. FairAid sends your answers to its Django backend, where a rule-based decision tree compares them with the criteria of the funding schemes registered in the system.

For example, a scheme may require a minimum NQF level, a particular field of study or an income below a specified limit. The engine checks the applicable conditions and produces a result for each registered scheme. This is a criteria check, not an application or a prediction of who will receive an award.

Go to eligibility screening ↗
Your results

What will your result tell you?

FairAid can classify a scheme as Likely eligible, Possibly eligible or Not eligible based on the rules it checks. Alongside the classification, you can see which criteria matched, which did not, and possible next steps.

A percentage shown in the results is the proportion of applicable criteria met. It is not your chance of receiving funding.

You can also request a plain-language explanation and ask follow-up questions about a saved screening. The AI assistant explains the decision tree's outcome; it cannot change the classification or make funding decisions. If the AI service is unavailable, the system can fall back to its rule-based explanation.

After screening

What should you do next?

When you sign in, your screening can be saved under My History, where you can return to previous results. Review the reasons and suggestions, then check the funding provider's official requirements and application instructions.

FairAid does not apply on your behalf, submit documents, approve loans or bursaries, or guarantee an award. The funding provider makes the final decision. Funding information can change, and some opportunities shown in Explore Funding are not yet supported by the screening engine.
